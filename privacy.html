Privacy Policy

How Mearns Gadget Repair protects your personal information and electronic data.

Home Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Mearns Gadget Repair ("we," "our," or "us"), located at 47 Raeside Wy, Newton Mearns, Glasgow G77 6YL, United Kingdom, is dedicated to upholding the privacy of our customers and website visitors. This policy sets out how we collect, handle, and protect personal details and device contents in full compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Device Data Security & Confidentiality

We understand that smart gadgets (phones, laptops, tablets, computers) contain sensitive personal and work data. When you submit a device for hardware or software repairs:

  • Our technicians will never browse, inspect, copy, or distribute your private photos, messages, browsing history, or documents.
  • We only access test menus, display diagnostics, cameras, and audio components required to verify functionality.
  • We always recommend that customers back up their device data before handing it over for service whenever possible.

3. Information We Collect

When booking a repair or submitting a quote request, we collect:

  • Customer name, contact phone number, and email address.
  • Device details (make, model, serial number/IMEI where necessary for parts verification, and fault description).
  • Delivery/pickup address for customers opting for local courier or postal repair services.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to provide quotes, communicate repair progress, furnish warranty support, and invoice completed services. We never sell, lease, or distribute customer details to third-party advertisers.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Lead

For questions regarding our privacy practices or data handling, contact us at info@mearnsgadgetrepair.co.uk or by phone at +44 7864 544448.

Our expert technicians specialize in repairing a wide range of devices, including laptops, smartwatches, computers, GHD, MacBooks, iPhones, and mobile phones.
