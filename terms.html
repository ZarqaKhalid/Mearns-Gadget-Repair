Terms & Conditions

Home Terms & Conditions

1. Repair Service Agreement

By submitting a device for diagnosis or repair to Mearns Gadget Repair (in-person, via courier pickup, or mail-in), you authorize our certified technicians to perform hardware diagnostics, disassemblies, component replacements, and necessary testing.

2. Free Diagnostics & "No Fix, No Fee"

We believe in honest and transparent service. If we evaluate your device and conclude that the fault is economically unviable or unrepairable due to severe PCB destruction, you will not be charged a repair fee under our "No Fix, No Fee" policy.

3. Comprehensive 90-Day Warranty

All hardware repairs (including replacement display screens, batteries, charging ports, HDMI sockets, and internal parts) include our 90-Day Warranty from the date of collection.

  • What is covered: Defective replacement components or technician workmanship issues that occur during normal daily operation.
  • What is excluded: Subsequent accidental drops, screen impact cracks, internal bleeding caused by external force, water submersion after repair, and unauthorized third-party tampering.

4. Customer Data Responsibility

While our technicians handle all customer hardware with maximum care and take precautions to prevent software alterations, Mearns Gadget Repair is not liable for existing data corruption or data loss resulting from pre-existing hardware failures (such as failing NAND memory or liquid-damaged storage chips). Customers are strongly encouraged to back up device data prior to repair.

5. Device Collection Policy

Devices completed must be collected or accepted via delivery within 60 days of notification unless alternative arrangements are agreed upon in writing.

6. Contact Us

For warranty claims or repair questions, please contact our Newton Mearns workshop at +44 7864 544448 or email info@mearnsgadgetrepair.co.uk.

Our expert technicians specialize in repairing a wide range of devices, including laptops, smartwatches, computers, GHD, MacBooks, iPhones, and mobile phones.
